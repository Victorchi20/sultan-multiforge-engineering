What Each Service Includes

Service 01

Structural & Architectural Designs

We provide comprehensive structural and architectural design services for all building types. Our licensed engineers produce detailed drawings, load calculations, and construction documents that meet regulatory requirements and are ready for immediate site use.

We use industry-standard tools including AutoCAD, ETABS, and STAAD.Pro to ensure accuracy, safety, and compliance at every level of the design process.

  • Full structural analysis & load calculations
  • Architectural floor plans, elevations & sections
  • Foundation design (raft, strip, pile)
  • Reinforced concrete & steel framing designs
  • Regulatory approval drawing packages
  • 3D visualisation & render on request

Service 02

Building Construction

We handle complete construction of both residential and commercial buildings. From site preparation and foundation works through to superstructure, roofing, and internal finishes — every phase is delivered to a high standard under our direct supervision.

  • Residential buildings — bungalows, duplexes, estates
  • Commercial buildings — offices, shops, warehouses
  • Foundation & substructure works
  • Reinforced concrete frame construction
  • Roofing, block work & plastering
  • Complete internal & external finishing

Service 03

Site / Project Management

Our project management service gives clients complete peace of mind — from the first day on site to final handover. We coordinate all contractors, manage timelines, control costs, and ensure work meets specification at every stage.

  • Full-time site supervision & quality control
  • Contractor procurement & management
  • Project scheduling & milestone tracking
  • Regular client progress reports
  • Cost monitoring & variation management
  • Health, safety & environmental compliance

Service 04

BOQ Estimation & Quantity Surveying

Accurate cost intelligence is the foundation of any successful construction project. Our quantity surveyors prepare detailed Bills of Quantities and cost estimates that give clients clear, reliable figures before committing to construction.

  • Detailed Bills of Quantities (BOQ) preparation
  • Pre-construction cost estimates & budgets
  • Tender pricing & contractor evaluation
  • Interim valuation & payment certificates
  • Final account preparation & settlement
  • Value engineering & cost optimisation
