Onyedikachi

Engr. Emmanuel Onyedikachi is the founder and sole engineer of {{ site_settings.company_name|default:"Sultan Multiforge Engineering Ltd" }}. Based in {{ site_settings.address|default:"Nsukka, Enugu State, Nigeria" }}, he is a hands-on professional who personally oversees every project from first consultation to final handover.

His expertise covers the full range of engineering and construction works — structural and architectural design, building construction for both residential and commercial clients, site and project management, BOQ estimation and quantity surveying, wall decoration (screening and painting), interior and exterior design, and construction equipment rentals.

What sets Engr. Emmanuel apart is simple: when you hire {{ site_settings.company_name|default:"Sultan Multiforge" }}, you get him — not a junior staff, not a subcontractor. He personally designs, supervises, and delivers. That level of personal accountability is rare, and it shows in the quality of every completed project.

Mission & Vision

My Mission

To deliver high-quality, honest, and affordable engineering and construction services — personally. Every client gets my full attention, my expertise, and my commitment to getting it right from start to finish.

My Vision

To be the most trusted engineering name in Nsukka and Enugu State — known not for the size of my firm, but for the quality of my work, the reliability of my word, and the lasting strength of every structure I build.
