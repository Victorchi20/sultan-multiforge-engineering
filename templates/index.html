About the Engineer

Engr. Emmanuel
Onyedikachi

Sultan Multiforge Engineering Ltd is the personal engineering practice of Engr. Emmanuel Onyedikachi, based in Nsukka, Enugu State. He handles every project personally — from the initial design and cost estimation to on-site supervision and final delivery.

With years of hands-on experience in structural design, building construction, quantity surveying, wall decoration, interior design, and equipment rentals, Engr. Emmanuel brings full engineering capability to every client he works with.

  • Personal attention on every project
  • Structural & Architectural Design
  • BOQ Estimation & Quantity Surveying
  • Timely Delivery & Cost Transparency
  • Interior, Exterior & Wall Decoration
